How to Watch UFC 325: Volkanovski vs Lopes 2 Live Stream

UFC 325: Volkanovski vs Lopes 2 takes place at Qudos Bank Arena in Sydney, Australia.

In the main event, two-time featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski defends his title against Diego Lopes in their rematch. Australia’s Volkanovski won their first fight in April 2025, defeating Brazilian-born Lopes of Mexico by unanimous decision.

In the co-main event, Dan Hooker of New Zealand and Benoit Saint Denis of France face off at lightweight.

What time to Start

Crackstreams UFC 325 airs live on Paramount+. The main card begins at 9:00 p.m. ET / 6:00 p.m. PT. The prelims start at 7:00 p.m. ET / 4:00 p.m. PT, and the early prelims kick off at 5:00 p.m. ET / 2:00 p.m. PT.
